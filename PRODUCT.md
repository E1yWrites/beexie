# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Pixel artists, game developers, and modders creating sprite, tile, or texture references.
- Designers creating retro avatars, editorial graphics, social assets, and posters.
- Makers crafting cross-stitch, bead, perler, and physical grid patterns.
- Visual artists wanting authentic, mathematically sound low-resolution pixel art.

## Product Purpose

Turn any image into genuine pixel art in seconds with fine-grained control over integer grid resolution, Median-Cut palette reduction, Floyd-Steinberg dithering, and multi-scale nearest-neighbor export. 100% client-side Canvas processing: zero server uploads, 100% private.

## Product Surfaces

1. **Landing Page:** A Spot-Ink Proof Studio front door over flowing Bezier `BackgroundPaths` ribbons, featuring an enlarged asymmetric two-column hero (`0.84fr / 1.36fr`) with crisp unitalicized display type, prominent `Open Pixel Studio` CTA, Cobalt-spotlight dropzone button, and a live interactive `[data-spotlight]` proof bench of `public/specimen.jpg` (`Compare | Pixel art | Original` switch, `Grid lines` toggle, `16x16` to `96x96` presets, Floyd-Steinberg dither switch, and 16 extracted swatches), followed by a framed 4-column hairline `[data-spotlight]` Pipeline Proof Sheet, a framed `0.84fr / 1.36fr` Split FAQ Accordion Sheet, and a bottom launch sheet.
2. **Pixel Art Studio:** A Dual-Theme Proof Bench (Light Cotton Ivory + Dark Pressroom Charcoal) pairing a slim `64px` `01-04` stage index rail with an expansive central Cobalt-spotlight proof bench, top mode/zoom controls, live status strip, spacing-grouped stage inspector, and dashed-rule Export ticket.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** **beexie** (lowercase geometric wordmark paired with the geometric ink-stamp bee emblem).
- **Specimen:** Existing CC0 photographic specimen (`public/specimen.jpg`, 1024x1024 koi pond).
- **Visual Direction:** Spot-Ink Proof Studio: Cotton-Press Ivory (`#f8f6f1` / `#ffffff`) in Light mode and Pressroom Charcoal (`#12141a` / `#191c23`) in Dark mode, Electric Cobalt (`#1e46e8` / `#2d55eb`) interactive actions and pointer-tracking Spotlight Card glow, and Vermilion (`#eb4a2d` / `#ff5a3c`) compare slider & live dither accent.
- **Animated Background:** Mirrored flowing cubic-Bezier `BackgroundPaths` curves (`Path2D` Canvas 2D) animating smoothly on Landing and holding calm static architectural framing in Studio.
- **Preview Equals Export:** The exact processed pixels render the preview and the encoded file.
- **Resolution is Real:** True area-averaged integer downsampling math, never a decorative CSS filter.
- **Accessibility & Contrast:** Verified WCAG AA contrast across Light Cotton Ivory and Dark Pressroom themes.
