- **Keep:** beexie name, bee mark, every feature claim that is true today, light + dark themes. Copy may be rewritten; no invented claims.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** The landing is the instruction booklet from the game box: a cover, numbered "how to play" spreads, labeled diagrams of real controls. It refuses the category default (dark panel, pixel-font headline, neon accent, before/after slider in a card).
- **OWN-WORLD:** Glossy white booklet stock, black ink, flat spot plates in red, yellow, and blue owning whole regions; sprites printed big at integer scale with leader-line callouts; ghost cell grids; no gradients, no glows, no rounded SaaS cards. Dark theme is the cartridge shell: charcoal plastic with the same plates as label art. Heavy Japanese-style gothic display, clean gothic body, a pixel face only for sprite captions and stage numerals.
- **STORY:** The visitor sees the koi printed as a real pixel sprite on the cover, reads four numbered panels that explain the pipeline with callouts on real controls, flips through retro presets rendered live, trusts the "stays in your browser" page, and opens the Studio or drops an image on the cover.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Booklet cover. Red plate owns the left 55 percent with the giant koi sprite (live pipeline output, integer scaled) breaking its edge; right column on white holds the headline, a two-line pitch, the black "Open Studio" cover button, and a drop target. A thin label band across the top carries the bee mark, nav, and theme toggle. Panel 1 of the "How to play" spread peeks at the bottom edge.
- **FORM:** The Instruction Booklet, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key `2524a50e`. Raises: one accent reserved for the stage in use (automata); flat plates only (linocut); the cover converts a dropped image in place (HyperCard); ghost grid and fixed readout slots (seven-segment); specimen owns the center, captions at the rim (accretion). Signature interaction: ghost grid fill, cells fill in scanline order when an image converts. Motion grammar: stepped, never eased glides; reduced motion is instant.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
